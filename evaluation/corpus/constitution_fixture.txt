PART III FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

Article 14. Equality before law.—The State shall not deny to any person equality before the law or the equal protection of the laws within the territory of India.

Article 19. Protection of certain rights regarding freedom of speech, etc.—(1) All citizens shall have the right—
(a) to freedom of speech and expression;
(b) to assemble peaceably and without arms;
(c) to form associations or unions.
(2) Nothing in sub-clause (a) of clause (1) shall prevent the State from making any law imposing reasonable restrictions on the exercise of the said right in the interests of the sovereignty and integrity of India, public order, decency or morality.

Article 21. Protection of life and personal liberty.—No person shall be deprived of his life or personal liberty except according to procedure established by law.
